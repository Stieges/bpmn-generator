# Layout Quality Metrics — Baseline

Generated by `scripts/bench/layout-metrics.mjs`. Not part of `npm test` — a snapshot for
`docs/layout-quality-analysis.md`, re-run on demand, not enforced as a regression gate.

**Columns.** *Crossings*: pairwise segment intersections between non-adjacent sequence-flow
edges. *Bends*: total direction changes, and how many edges need at most one. *Diagonals*:
non-orthogonal segments — always expected to be 0. *Area*: node-bbox width×height and px² per
node. *Aspect*: bbox w/h. *Chain alignment*: of the links that could structurally be straight,
how many share center-y within 1 px. *Excluded*: links left out of that quota because a
vertical offset is correct for them — see below. *Edge-through-node*: segments crossing a
foreign node box.

**Reading the numbers.** Two comparisons are invalid and the table deliberately does not
invite them. Area must not be compared between "ELK raw" and the pipeline rows: raw ELK has
not placed lane bands yet, so the growth measures the existence of swimlanes, not bloat. And
area must not be read as a quality score for a wrapped layout: wrapping trades width for
height on purpose, which grows total area while improving aspect ratio, which is why aspect
has its own column.

**Alignment exclusions.** A link is only counted when its two endpoints could legitimately
share a center-y. Excluded are: `cross-lane` (lanes are horizontal bands — such a link can
never be straight), `fold-back` (target left of source: a loop, or a row fold in a wrapped
layout), `branch` (target is a split or source is a join — ELK puts it on its branches'
barycenter, which is what makes those branches straight), `hosts-boundary` (the source carries
a boundary event, whose flow ELK re-anchors onto the host, producing the same barycenter
effect), and `boundary-src` (the source is a boundary event, pinned to its host's edge).
Counting these as misalignment is exactly the error the first version of this harness made.
